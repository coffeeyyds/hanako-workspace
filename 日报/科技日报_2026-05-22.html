🪦 MATLAB 之父 Cleve Moler 去世

📅 2026-05-22📰 Hacker News · 177 评论
📋 中文解读

Cleve Moler 是谁？他是 MATLAB 的创造者。MATLAB 你可能没用过，但你坐的飞机、用的手机、看到的天气预报，背后都有它。它是过去四十年全球工程师和科学家的「通用计算器」。

Moler 在 1970 年代用手写了 MATLAB 的第一个版本——当时只是为了让学生更容易地使用他教的一门线性代数课。后来他和朋友创办 MathWorks 公司，一辈子没上市、没炒作、没上过头条。他活成了硅谷神话的反面：不喧嚣，但留下的东西比大多数百亿独角兽都重。

为什么值得记住：在一个人人追求增长和 IPO 的时代，Moler 证明了另一种可能——安静地做真正有用的工具，然后让它影响全世界。

📄 原文

Cleve Moler, founder of MathWorks and creator of MATLAB — the computational backbone of global engineering and science for over four decades — has passed away. Moler wrote the first version of MATLAB by hand in the 1970s as a teaching tool for his linear algebra students. MathWorks, the company he co-founded, never went public, never chased hype, and remained privately held throughout his lifetime. His legacy quietly underpins everything from aircraft design to weather forecasting.

Hacker News commenters noted: "MATLAB is one of those pieces of software that runs the modern world but almost nobody outside of engineering knows about."

💰 Anthropic 首次盈利 vs OpenAI 巨亏

📅 2026-05-22📰 36氪 · 新智元
📋 中文解读

这是 AI 行业今年最重要的财务对比。

Anthropic（Claude 的母公司）历史上第一次实现了盈利。它没有像 OpenAI 那样烧钱铺市场、补贴用户、打价格战，而是走了一条更克制的路：控制模型规模、优化推理效率、主攻企业和开发者市场。

OpenAI的同期财报则显示：每收入 1 美元，要亏掉 1.22 美元。这是一个危险的数字——意味着即使在 ChatGPT 拥有数亿用户的情况下，OpenAI 的商业模式仍然无法覆盖其运营成本。

为什么这很重要：它揭示了 AI 行业的一个核心张力。烧钱换市场的路径在互联网时代有效（先免费圈用户，再通过广告/增值赚钱），但 AI 不同——每多一个用户，GPU 成本就实实在在地增加。没有边际成本递减效应。Anthropic 正在证明「克制」也可以赢。

📄 原文

For the first time in its history, Anthropic — the maker of Claude — has become profitable. The milestone comes even as the company continues to invest heavily in frontier model research.

Meanwhile, OpenAI's latest quarterly filing reveals a stark contrast: for every $1 in revenue, the company loses $1.22. Despite ChatGPT's hundreds of millions of users, the unit economics remain deeply negative.

Industry analysts note that AI companies face a structural challenge that internet-era platforms didn't: GPU inference costs scale linearly with usage. There is no zero-marginal-cost effect. Anthropic's more measured approach — targeting enterprise customers, optimizing inference efficiency, and avoiding a race to the bottom on pricing — appears to be yielding results.

📱 AI 正在「抢」你手机的内存

📅 2026-05-22📰 Hacker News · 132 分
📋 中文解读

这是一个「看不见的手」正在改变你生活成本的案例。

发生了什么：三星和 SK 海力士——两家控制全球 70% DRAM 市场的韩国公司——正在把最先进的生产线从做手机内存转向做 AI 服务器需要的 HBM（高带宽内存）。一块 HBM 售价是普通内存的 3-5 倍。

对你的影响：便宜的智能手机和笔记本电脑会越来越难买到。2026 年下半年低端手机物料成本预计上涨 8-12%。一款售价 150 美元的手机，这个涨幅足以吃掉全部利润。厂商可能直接放弃这个价位。

更深的含义：这是 AI 繁荣的「负外部性」。AI 公司在赚钱，普通人在买单。每一块 3000 美元的 HBM 去训练 GPT-6 的时候，就有几十部廉价手机因为缺内存变得更贵或干脆消失。历史类比：2017 年加密货币挖矿让显卡价格翻了一倍，但这次的规模和影响范围远超那次。

📄 原文

An invisible force is repricing consumer electronics. It isn't tariffs. It isn't inflation. It's AI.

Samsung and SK Hynix — controlling roughly 70% of the global DRAM market — are redirecting their most advanced fabrication capacity away from the memory chips used in smartphones and laptops, toward the High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) demanded by AI data centers.

The economics are straightforward. An HBM module sells for 3-5x the price of an equivalent-capacity standard DRAM chip. When NVIDIA and AMD are lining up to buy every HBM chip you can manufacture at a premium, reserving capacity for consumer electronics is commercially self-defeating.

The result: supply of standard DRAM is contracting while demand hasn't decreased. Prices are rising. Industry analysts project an 8-12% increase in bill-of-materials costs for budget smartphones in the second half of 2026. For a $150 device, that wipes out the entire profit margin. Some manufacturers may exit this price tier entirely.

This is a textbook case of negative externality: the AI industry is generating enormous value while offloading costs onto the most vulnerable link in the supply chain — everyday consumers.

🧠 李飞飞再出手：空间智能的「ImageNet」来了

📅 2026-05-22📰 量子位
📋 中文解读
